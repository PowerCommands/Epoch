# Modern African nations

Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa use the standard nation and leader registries. Each has one default and one alternative; scenario selection and Overthrow Leadership resolve both through the existing nation identity. City names, editor catalogs and audio aliases are generated by `npm run generate-manifests`.

| Nation | Default | Alternative | Gameplay transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nigeria | Bola Tinubu | Goodluck Jonathan | Commercial development and high resource-deal interest → stronger institutions, diplomacy and peace |
| Kenya | William Ruto | Uhuru Kenyatta | Agricultural growth and enterprise → infrastructure and more credible defensive strength |
| South Africa | Nelson Mandela | Thabo Mbeki | Reconciliation, culture and international cooperation → economic development and regional diplomacy |

These are gameplay interpretations of the requested identities. Ideologies use Epoch's existing vocabulary, rather than assigning real-world party labels. Dialogue is original fictional writing, not historical quotations. Games of Nations preferences are game flavor, not assertions about the leaders' personal favorite sports.

All six use existing national agendas, military doctrines, covert profiles and era strategies. Opportunistic attacks on weak nations and impulsive bullying are disabled. Mandela's low aggression and expansion, high peace preference, cultural agenda and cultural defense doctrine favor reconciliation while retaining defensive capability. War dialogue covers every required reason because scenario overrides and unusual game circumstances can still produce war.

Tinubu's maximum resource exploitation interest, economic agenda and coastal trade strategy express his resource-development identity; oil and natural gas also appear in his diplomacy. The existing leader schema has no individual commodity preference field. No new resource-targeting system is introduced.

All three nations set `audioPlaylistId: 'nation_mali-empire'`, the actual shared folder key. The sound manifest exposes the same existing MP3 paths under each new nation's key; there are no copied audio files.

## Assets

Each leader has an AI-generated painted portrait (416 × 416 PNG) and a diplomacy room (2048 × 872 WebP). Artwork was generated for this addition and visually checked alongside the existing painted leader style. These are artistic interpretations, not archival photographs.

Flag SVG sources from Wikimedia Commons were rasterized to the existing 128-pixel flag height, preserving national aspect ratios:

- [Nigeria flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Nigeria.svg): 256 × 128 PNG.
- [Kenya flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Kenya.svg): 192 × 128 PNG.
- [South Africa flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_South_Africa.svg): 192 × 128 PNG.

## Historical references

Biographical descriptions distinguish completed tenures from inauguration dates, avoiding claims about who currently holds office.

- [Bola Tinubu biography](https://ndnb.ng/biography/bola-tinubu): Lagos governorship, 1999–2007.
- [Goodluck Jonathan biography, ACCORD](https://www.accord.org.za/people/goodluck-ebele-jonathan/): presidency and election-observation work.
- [Commonwealth observer mission](https://thecommonwealth.org/news/secretary-general-meets-dr-goodluck-jonathan): Jonathan's 2015 Tanzania mission.
- [Kenyan presidency biography](https://www.president.go.ke/administration/office-of-the-president/): Ruto's preceding offices.
- [Kenyan presidential library](https://presidentiallibrary.go.ke/presidents/he-dr-william-samoei-ruto): Ruto's agricultural and housing programmes.
- [Uhuru Kenyatta presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Uhuru_Kenyatta): tenure, infrastructure and regional policy.
- [South African presidency history](https://www.presidency.gov.za/history): Mandela and Mbeki's terms.
- [Mandela biography, South African government](https://www.gov.za/mandela100/biography): transition to democracy and reconciliation.
- [Thabo Mbeki biography, Mandela Foundation](https://www.nelsonmandela.org/thabo-mbeki-bio): succession and political career.

## Validation

`npm run test:modern-africa` checks canonical defaults, reusable profile IDs, editor configuration, scenario/save round trips, leader-specific war dialogue, portraits and flags, generated catalogs, Mali playlist reuse, and actual capitulation-driven leader replacement in both directions. `EPOCH_VERIFY_BUILD=1` additionally verifies that the built asset files match their public sources.

Validation performed for this addition:

- Standard production build and typecheck passed; the existing large-bundle warning remains.
- All nine modern Africa content tests passed, including built-asset equality.
- The Overthrow Leadership suite passed, including both directions for each new pair.
- A headless Chrome check of the built editor created a scenario with all three nations, selected both leaders for each nation, decoded every new image, and fetched the shared audio URLs successfully.
- Existing nation/leader content and Games of Nations checks passed except for three known baseline failures, reproduced against the original commit: the roster audit expects three English leaders but has four; the global war-dialogue catalog lacks entries for some older leaders; and the random-scenario UI test expects a different menu ordering. The historical roster audit remains unchanged, and its test now accounts separately for the three newly added nations.
