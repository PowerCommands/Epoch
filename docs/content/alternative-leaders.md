# Alternative leadership for every playable nation

The roster contains 86 leaders across 41 playable nations. Twenty-seven requested alternatives were added only to nations that previously had one leader. Every nation now has at least two leaders; England, France, the United States and Germany retain three. No playable nation remains below the minimum. Barbarians are outside the playable nation registry.

A comparison against a snapshot taken before this task confirmed that all 59 pre-existing leader configurations and all nation definitions are unchanged. Defaults and scenario starting governments are preserved. The preceding Belarus/Yugoslavia/Czechoslovakia addition was retained and included in this audit.

Every addition has an explicit non-default identity, biography, title, ideology, agenda, doctrine, covert profile, opportunism/impulsivity settings, culture priorities, Games preferences, all ten personality values, seven diplomacy flavor fields and ten war-declaration phrases. The 270 new war lines are fictional dialogue selected only after the normal AI chooses war. Sport preferences are game design choices, not historical assertions.

The full configurations intentionally differ from their nation's existing leader. Barbarossa and Sundiata emphasize military consolidation and expansion. Gorbachev emphasizes reform, trade and diplomacy; Kublai and Suleiman emphasize sustainable imperial administration. Tsai combines technology and trade with a modern defensive army. Tokugawa emphasizes durable defensive consolidation. Anne Bonny retains opportunistic maritime raiding but has stronger economic interest, lower loss tolerance, no Impulsive Bully behavior and a three-city preference cap rather than Mad Jack's one-port limit.

Kekkonen favors conservative neutrality and negotiated balance rather than Stubb's globalist defense agenda. Harper emphasizes conservative economic and security policy; Fox favors liberal trade and a lean military; Perón favors national state development and mobilization. Poroshenko has an elite army, stronger nationalism and less diplomatic emphasis than Zelenskyy. Pahlavi's modernization and international commerce contrast with Khomeini's revolutionary traditionalism. Faisal I emphasizes institution-building; Rabin pairs prepared defense with negotiated settlement. Atatürk emphasizes secular institutional modernization; King Faisal shifts toward traditional statecraft and regional diplomacy; Thaksin emphasizes domestic growth; Park pairs industrial development with military mobilization. Tsikhanouskaya favors democratic opening, Peter II a conservative monarchy, and Havel cultural and liberal international cooperation.

Opportunism is enabled for Barbarossa, Sundiata and Anne Bonny and disabled for the others. Impulsive Bully is disabled for all 27. These settings describe game behavior, not private psychological claims. Han Seo-jin's biography explicitly states she is a fictional character created for Epoch; her original portrait was requested without any real-person likeness reference. Tsikhanouskaya's biography explicitly frames her presidential government as a game alternative.

Historical framing follows the requested roster. Supplementary checks included [Kekkonen's neutrality](https://www.presidentti.fi/ahtisaari/puheet-1994/p9409.uke.html), [Havel's presidency](https://www.vaclavhavel.cz/cs/vaclav-havel/prezident) and [Peter II's wartime exile](https://royalfamily.org/dinasty/history-of-the-dynasty/). Medieval portraits without reliable contemporary likenesses are historical reconstructions.

## Existing audio limitation

Mali already has a folder/manifest key `nation_mali-empire`, while its nation ID is `nation_mali_empire`. The existing music manager consequently uses the `start` playlist fallback for both Mansa Musa and Sundiata Keita. This pre-existing mismatch was preserved under the instruction not to alter national audio. It does not affect leader selection or playability. All other new alternatives resolve their nation's existing playlist directly, including the established shared mappings.
