Australia content notes
=======================

John Howard is the default; Bob Hawke is the alternative. Both use the economic agenda, a five-city voluntary settlement cap, high resource exploitation interest, and coastal era strategies. Howard shifts from Coastal Foundation to Defensive Builder, with Naval Power doctrine, Conservatism and Pragmatist covert behavior. Hawke retains Coastal Tech Economy, with Defensive Modern doctrine, Globalism and Merchant covert behavior. Globalism's trade and diplomacy bonuses fit the requested economic integration emphasis.

Defensive alliances activate automatically for both leaders. Existing joint-war acceptance evaluates trust, alliance membership and relative military strength; it has no leader war-tolerance input. Howard's larger military budget improves his ability to join those wars, and his higher war tolerance and casualty thresholds support sustained commitments. No Australian exceptions were added to these systems. Preferences are tendencies, subject to normal AI threat and economy safeguards.

Biographical references: [John Howard, National Museum of Australia](https://www.nma.gov.au/explore/features/prime-ministers/john-howard), [John Howard, National Archives](https://www.naa.gov.au/explore-collection/australias-prime-ministers/john-howard), [Bob Hawke, National Museum of Australia](https://www.nma.gov.au/explore/features/prime-ministers/bob-hawke). Dialogue and sports choices are fictional gameplay flavor.

Assets use the built-in image generation tool. Portraits were resized to 416×416 PNG; audience scenes to 2048×872 WebP. Files are in `public/assets/sprites/leaders/` as `john-howard.png`, `john-howard-room.webp`, `bob-hawke.png`, and `bob-hawke-room.webp`. The flag follows the existing editable SVG/raster convention at `public/assets/sprites/flags/australia.svg` and `australia.png`, with the national 2:1 proportions (900×450), Commonwealth Star and Southern Cross. Audio aliases England's playlist without copied tracks.
